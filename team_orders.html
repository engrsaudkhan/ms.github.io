Season planning

Order earlier than the deadline pressure.

A club order becomes messy when design approval, sample sizing and roster collection happen at the same time. Separate those steps.

  • Approve the design before collecting final rosters
  • Use samples or a size guide before locking sizes
  • Set one internal deadline for every team manager
  • Keep one final roster file as the source of truth
