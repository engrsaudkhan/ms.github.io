Sizing preparation

Use a fitting process, not assumptions.

This starter guide helps a team manager organize sizing. Official garment measurements must be confirmed for the selected product before ordering.

IMPORTANTSizes vary by product and cut. This page is a process guide, not an official measurement chart.

Fitting checklist

Record the size while the player is wearing the sample.

01

Wear normal base layers

Fit the uniform over the clothing the player normally uses for that sport.

02

Check movement

Raise arms, bend, run and sit. A size that looks fine while standing may restrict play.

03

Check length and width

Do not choose based on chest width alone. Body length and sleeve length also matter.

04

Write the final choice

Record product and size together because different items may need different sizes.
